Delete Your Expense Tracker Account

Expense Tracker is developed by Vipul Bajaj. You can delete your account and all associated data in either of the following ways.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Expense Tracker and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete All Data and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email vipulbjj@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Expense Tracker account deletion". Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, email, and Google account link).
  • All transactions, accounts, balances, categories, budgets, bills, subscriptions, and settings.
  • When deleting from the app: all data cached on your device, including detected payment notifications.

No data is retained after deletion, apart from anything we are legally required to keep. Routine Firebase backups, if any, expire automatically.
