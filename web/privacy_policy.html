This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Expense Tracker application.

The words of which the initial letter is capitalized have meanings defined under the following conditions. The following definitions shall have the same meaning regardless of whether they appear in singular or in plural.

  • Application means the software program provided by the Company downloaded by You on any electronic device, named Expense Tracker.
  • You means the individual accessing or using the Service, or the company, or other legal entity on behalf of which such individual is accessing or using the Service, as applicable.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

While using Our Application, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Email address (via Google Sign-In)
  • First name and last name (via Google Sign-In)

Financial Data

The Application allows You to track Your expenses, income, accounts, budgets, and bills. This data is:

  • Stored in the cloud (Google Cloud Firestore) under Your account so it can sync across Your devices, and cached locally on Your device.
  • Protected in transit (TLS) and at rest by Google Firebase. Sensitive fields such as amounts, balances, categories, and notes are additionally encrypted by the Application before upload.
  • Accessible only to Your signed-in account, and never sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Payment Notifications (Optional)

If You turn on automatic payment detection, the Application asks for Android notification access and reads notifications on Your device to recognise payments from banking and payment apps (for example Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or Your bank).

  • This feature is off by default and is only enabled after You agree to an in-app disclosure and grant access in Android settings. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Notifications that do not look like payments are ignored and not stored.
  • For detected payments, the amount, payee/merchant, time, source app, and original notification text are stored only on Your device until You approve or dismiss them.
  • When You approve a detected payment, it is saved as a transaction to Your account in the cloud. The original notification text is not uploaded.
  • This data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Usage of Third-Party Network Services

  • Currency rates: the Application downloads exchange rates from the Frankfurter API (api.frankfurter.app). No personal or financial data is sent; the service can see Your IP address as part of the request.
  • Fonts: the Application may download fonts from Google Fonts. No personal data is sent beyond the standard request information such as IP address.

Biometric Data (Local Auth)

The Application offers a fast sign-in feature using biometric authentication (fingerprint or face ID) provided by Your device.

  • This processing happens exclusively on Your device.
  • The Application does not collect, store, or transmit Your biometric data to any servers.
  • We simply receive a "pass/fail" signal from the operating system to unlock the App.

Retention of Your Personal Data

We will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use Your Personal Data to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our legal agreements and policies.

Delete Your Data

You can delete Your account and all associated data at any time from the Application's settings (Delete All Data). This permanently removes Your transactions, accounts, budgets, bills, and settings from our servers, deletes Your sign-in account, and clears the data stored on Your device.

If You no longer have the Application installed, You can request deletion of Your account and data by emailing vipulbjj@gmail.com from the Google account You used to sign in, with the subject "Expense Tracker account deletion". We will delete Your account and all associated data within 30 days. See also our account deletion page.
